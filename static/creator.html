Hi, I'm Keith.

I'm Keith Lim Guang Rong, the creator of HawkerFit.

HawkerFit is a side project I built for fun. I wanted an easier way to answer the daily "makan where?" question: find good food nearby, check which hawker centres are actually open, and know roughly what's in local dishes, whether you're watching your macros or just hungry.

It's free, it's a work in progress, and I'm always happy to hear ideas for what to add next.
