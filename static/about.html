Eat well at the hawker centre.

HawkerFit is a free AI food guide for Singapore. It helps you decide what to eat, find hawker centres that are actually open, and understand the calories and macros in local dishes.

Open HawkerFit Meet the creator

What it does

Ask in plain English"Cheap supper near Bedok, not spicy" gets you three dish-and-place picks.
Scan food or a menuCalories, protein, carbs, fat and sodium, plus what to order for cutting or bulking.
Know before you goOfficial cleaning-closure dates and live rain forecasts for every hawker centre.
Can't decide? SpinA wheel picks an open hawker centre near you, with walking and driving estimates.

Where the data comes from

Hawker centres, photos & closuresNational Environment Agency (NEA), via data.gov.sg, under the Singapore Open Data Licence
WeatherNEA 2-hour weather forecast, via data.gov.sg (refreshed every 10 minutes)
Famous dishes & supper spotsHawkerFit's own notes on popular centres
Recommendations & nutritionGenerated by AI (Claude, by Anthropic)

Please note

  • Nutrition values are AI estimates for a typical portion and can vary by stall by about 20%. They are not medical or dietary advice.
  • Prices are typical hawker prices, not quotes from specific stalls.
  • Travel times are rough estimates based on distance. Use the directions links for exact routes.
  • Halal and dietary notes are guidance only. Always check with the stall.
  • Stalls change often, so a recommended dish may not be available.

Privacy

  • No sign-up and no account. HawkerFit does not ask for your name or email.
  • Your location (if you share it) is kept in your own browser and is only used to sort results. The server does not save it.
  • Photos you scan and questions you ask are sent to the AI service to get an answer, and are not stored by HawkerFit.
  • To keep the service free, each visitor gets a limited number of AI searches per day.
